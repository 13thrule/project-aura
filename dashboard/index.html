Node color: real alpha(8-13Hz)/beta(13-30Hz) FFT power balance per channel (blue = alpha-dominant/calm, magenta = beta-dominant/aroused), computed via direct DFT over each channel's most recent window — same power-per-band method as pipeline/aura_pipeline/features.py's band_power(), at a coarser frequency resolution (fixed 1Hz-spaced sample points, not every FFT bin).
